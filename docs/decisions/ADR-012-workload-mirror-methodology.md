# ADR-012: Workload Mirror Methodology & Human-to-AI Labor Mapping

## Status
**APPROVED** (Phase 0 Addendum)

## Context
Solo developers or small engineering teams frequently carry a massive, multifaceted workload spanning diverse disciplines (frontend, backend, DevOps, CMS maintenance, graphic design, user support). Communicating the true scale of this workload to stakeholders or estimating the AI workforce needed to relieve the human developer is challenging.

## Decision
We introduce the **Workload Mirror** analytical framework:
1. Deconstructs the human developer's discrete responsibilities into functional domains.
2. Maps these responsibilities to an **Equivalent AI Workforce Simulation**.
3. Calculates the estimated virtual compensation and operational cloud costs required to field that equivalent digital team.
4. Preserves historical monthly snapshots to monitor workload changes over time.

## Rationale
- Provides transparency into developer overextension and highlights specific functional areas prime for autonomous delegation.
- Offers an objective, data-driven framework for scaling digital workforce capacity.

## Consequences
- **Positive:** Powerful operational insight, clear delegation roadmap, objective visualization of engineering leverage.
- **Negative:** Methodological guardrails are mandatory; the system must not imply that human wages and AI virtual compensation are 1:1 interchangeable.
