# Salary Benchmark Source Registry

## 1. Registered Real-World Sources (2026 Baseline)

### 1. BPS Provinsi Jawa Tengah (Tier A)
- **Source ID**: `src_bps_jateng_2026`
- **Name**: Indikator Upah Tenaga Kerja Sektor Informasi & Komunikasi
- **Provider**: Badan Pusat Statistik (BPS)
- **URL**: `https://jateng.bps.go.id`
- **Reliability Tier**: TIER_A (Official Government / Statistical Data)
- **Methodology**: Sakernas representative cross-sectional survey of net monthly compensation in Central Java.
- **Coverage**: Graphic Designer, Content Publishing Specialist, Regional Media Operators.

### 2. Glints Indonesia (Tier B)
- **Source ID**: `src_glints_2026`
- **Name**: Glints Tech Talent & Salary Report 2026
- **Provider**: Glints Indonesia
- **URL**: `https://employers.glints.id/salary-report`
- **Reliability Tier**: TIER_B (Recognized Recruitment & Placement Survey)
- **Methodology**: Statistical analysis of 1,200+ employer salary disclosures and confirmed hiring transactions.
- **Coverage**: WordPress / CMS Specialist, Social Media Specialist, Fullstack Software Engineers.

### 3. Michael Page Indonesia (Tier B)
- **Source ID**: `src_page_2026`
- **Name**: Michael Page Indonesia Salary Benchmark Guide 2026
- **Provider**: Michael Page International
- **URL**: `https://www.michaelpage.co.id/salary-guide`
- **Reliability Tier**: TIER_B (Executive & Technical Search Report)
- **Methodology**: Proprietary corporate client placement database across Indonesian tech firms.
- **Coverage**: Systems Architects, Technical Leads, Principal Engineering.

### 4. Jobstreet by SEEK (Tier C)
- **Source ID**: `src_jobstreet_2026`
- **Name**: Jobstreet by SEEK Indonesia Salary Insights 2026
- **Provider**: Jobstreet / SEEK Group
- **URL**: `https://www.jobstreet.co.id/career-advice/salary-guide`
- **Reliability Tier**: TIER_C (Major Job Portal with Employer Disclosures)
- **Methodology**: Median extraction of employer-posted base salaries in Central Java and Remote Indonesia.
- **Coverage**: Web Administrator, IT Support Specialist.
