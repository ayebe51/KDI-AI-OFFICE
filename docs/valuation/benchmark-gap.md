# Benchmark Gap Analysis Specification

## 1. Mathematical Definition
The **Illustrative Benchmark Gap** quantifies the differential between the illustrative replacement workforce valuation and the actual baseline compensation:

$$\text{Illustrative Benchmark Gap} = \text{Illustrative Workforce Value (Median)} - \text{Actual Compensation (Total Monthly)}$$

### Canonical Demonstration
- **Illustrative Workforce Value (Median)**: Rp 15,580,000 / month
- **Actual Human Total Compensation**: Rp 4,500,000 / month (Base: Rp 3.8M, Allowance: Rp 700K)
- **Illustrative Benchmark Gap**: **Rp 11,080,000 / month**
- **Annualized Benchmark Gap**: **Rp 132,960,000 / year**

---

## 2. Mandatory Terminology & Governance Rules
To maintain absolute compliance and prevent legal or ethical liability:
- **PERMITTED**: *Illustrative Benchmark Gap*, *Estimated Workforce Replacement Differential*, *Workload-to-Market Comparison*.
- **STRICTLY PROHIBITED**: *Underpayment*, *Wage Theft*, *Unpaid Overtime*, *Illegal Compensation*, *Exploited Worker*.
