# Workload Valuation Model & Formulas

## 1. Valuation Mathematics
The valuation of a functional responsibility is derived from the verified market salary benchmark adjusted by effective workload allocation:

$$\text{Effective FTE}_i = \frac{\text{Allocation \%}_i}{100} \times (1 - \text{Overlap Factor}_i)$$

$$\text{Monthly Role Value}_i = \text{Benchmark Median}_i \times \text{Effective FTE}_i$$

$$\text{Total Illustrative Workforce Value} = \sum_{i=1}^{N} \text{Monthly Role Value}_i$$

$$\text{Annualized Illustrative Value} = \text{Total Monthly Value} \times 12$$
