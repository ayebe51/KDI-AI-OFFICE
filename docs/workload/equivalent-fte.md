# Equivalent FTE (Full-Time Equivalent) Specification

## 1. Definition & Boundaries
**Equivalent FTE** represents the aggregate volume of standard 40-hour full-time equivalent capacity required to reproduce the functional output delivered by an operator.

### Non-Goal Disclaimer
An Equivalent FTE calculation of **3.2 FTE** does **NOT** mean:
- That the organization is legally mandated to immediately hire 3.2 separate full-time employees.
- That the current operator must be paid 3.2 times the statutory minimum wage.

It **DOES** mean:
- That the functional breath and cognitive switching across 6 disciplines corresponds to the standard workload of ~3 full-time roles in an established technology and media organization.
- That replacing this single operator would require distributing tasks across multiple specialized personnel.
