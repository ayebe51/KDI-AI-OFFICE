# Workload Mirror Architecture

## 1. Concept: One Human to Multiple Functions
The **Workload Mirror** solves the fundamental discrepancy between formal organizational job titles and actual operational output:

```text
ONE HUMAN OPERATOR
    ↓
Multiple Responsibilities (Website Admin, CMS Dev, Graphic Design, Social Media, Decrees, IT Helpdesk)
    ↓
Multiple Normalized Market Roles (Web Admin, WP Specialist, Graphic Designer, Social Media Specialist, Content Specialist, IT Support)
    ↓
Verified Market Salary Benchmarks (BPS, Glints, Jobstreet SEEK)
    ↓
Double-Counting & Overlap Mitigation (0.1 Overlap Factor)
    ↓
Equivalent Headcount Capacity: 3.2 FTE
    ↓
Illustrative Replacement Workforce Value: Rp 15,580,000 / month
```

---

## 2. Evidence-Based Responsibility Modeling
A responsibility must be substantiated by empirical activity records:
- **Git commits**: Source code, theme tweaks, DNS record updates.
- **Server logs / Cloudflare audits**: Domain and SSL renewals, incident mitigation.
- **Creative archives**: Vector graphics, exported banners, print collaterals.
- **Task queues / CMS changelogs**: Published articles, scheduled social media feeds, student decrees.
- **Helpdesk receipts**: Workstation maintenance, LAN cabling, printer networking.
