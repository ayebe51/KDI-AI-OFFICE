# KDI Weekly Operations Report

## 1. Overview
The `WeeklyOperationsReport` aggregates 7-day office telemetry:
- **Objectives**: Total active, completed, and blocked strategic goals.
- **Tasks**: Completed, failed, and in-flight tasks.
- **Incidents**: Open vs resolved operational incidents.
- **Agent Activity**: Per-agent workload, active hours, and success rates.
- **AI Operating Cost**: Segregated LLM token inference, tools, and cloud infrastructure.
- **Important Decisions**: Audit log of human approvals and high-impact interventions.
- **Next Actions**: Actionable recommendations for upcoming sprints.
